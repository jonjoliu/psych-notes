PSYC 314 · Lecture 03 · Exam Study Guide 考点整理

Coping & Stress Management

Coping · Social Support · HAI · Nature · Mindfulness

A Overview 概览
Core Definition 核心定义

Coping = the process by which people manage the perceived discrepancy between demands and resources in a stressful situation. 应对 = 管理压力情境中需求与资源之间感知差距的过程

Key: it is a dynamic, transactional process — continuous appraisals and reappraisals, not a single event. 关键：这是一个动态的交互过程，而非单次事件

Transactional Model (Lazarus, 1984) 交互模型
  • Primary appraisal — Is this situation threatening? 初级评估：这有威胁吗？
  • Secondary appraisal — Do I have the resources to cope? 次级评估：我有资源应对吗？
  • Stimulus → Appraisal → Coping Response → loops back 刺激→评估→应对→循环反馈
  • Both appraisals feed into each other continuously 两种评估持续相互影响
Key point: coping behaviour ≠ coping outcome. Don't confound the two! 应对行为 ≠ 应对结果，不要混淆！
Four Functions of Coping 四类应对功能
  • Problem-focused — direct efforts to solve the problem 问题聚焦：直接解决问题
  • Emotion-focused — manage emotions generated by the stressor 情绪聚焦：管理压力引发的情绪
  • Relationship-focused — maintain social relationships during stress (DeLongis & O'Brien, 1990) 关系聚焦：维护社会关系
  • Meaning-focused — draw on beliefs/values to sustain coping (Folkman, 2008) 意义聚焦：借助信念/价值观维持应对
Approach vs. Avoidance 趋近 vs. 回避
  • Approach coping — actively deal with the problem or manage tension 趋近：积极处理问题或管理紧张
  • Avoidance coping — distance oneself; not think about it 回避：远离问题，不去思考
  • Both can be cognitive or behavioural 两种均可是认知层面或行为层面
  • No one strategy is best — depends on controllability of stressor 无最优策略——取决于压力源的可控性
✓ Avoidance better if uncontrollable; Approach better if control is possible (Roth & Cohen, 1986) 不可控→回避更好；可控→趋近更好
Learning Objectives 学习目标
  • Define coping and its primary functions (with examples) 定义应对及其主要功能（含举例）
  • Distinguish approach vs. avoidance coping 区分趋近与回避应对
  • Discuss effective strategies: disclosure, positive reappraisal, resilience 有效策略：披露、正向重评、韧性
  • Distinguish social support vs. social networks; buffering hypothesis 社会支持vs.社交网络；压力缓冲假说
  • Name types of social support; identify maladaptive forms 命名支持类型；识别不良支持形式
  • Describe HAI research findings and mechanisms 描述HAI研究发现与机制
  • Discuss nature's role in physical and mental health 自然对身心健康的作用
  • Discuss meditation and mindfulness as stress management 冥想与正念作为压力管理手段
Recommended Coping Strategies 推荐的应对策略

① Deal with things / problem-solve 处理问题/问题解决  ·  ② Process and express your feelings 处理并表达情感

③ Engage positive emotions; find benefits/meaning 调动积极情绪；寻找益处/意义  ·  ④ Accommodate to the stressor (acceptance) 接受压力源

⑤ Find support and collaborate 寻求支持与合作  ·  ⑥ Interact with animals · spend time in nature · meditate 与动物互动·接触自然·冥想

B Coping Types in Detail 应对类型详解
Problem-Focused Coping 问题聚焦应对
Active
  • Changing the situation: thinking of options, weighing pros & cons 改变情况：考虑选项，权衡利弊
  • Making a plan of action / formulating a list 制定行动计划
  • Increasing efforts to make things work 加倍努力使事情成功
  • Changing ourselves: seeking information, learning new skills 改变自己：获取信息，学习新技能
  • Taking a course / reading a book 上课/读书
Emotion-Focused Coping 情绪聚焦应对
Emotion
  • Denial — "I told myself it didn't happen" 否认
  • Escape-Avoidance — avoiding thinking about the problem 逃避回避
  • Wishful Thinking — wishing it would go away 幻想
  • Positive reappraisal — focusing on the bright side (adaptive) 正向重评（适应性）
  • Emotional expression — telling others 情绪表达
  • Self-care, prayer, social comparison, substance use 自我照顾、祈祷、社会比较、物质使用
Relationship-Focused Coping 关系聚焦应对
Relational
  • Empathic responding — trying to understand how other person felt 移情回应
  • Perspective taking — seeing things from person's perspective 换位思考
  • Support seeking — calling a friend for help 寻求支持
  • Support provision — trying to help the other person involved 提供支持
  • Compromise — finding a fair solution for all 妥协：寻找公平解决方案
  • Confrontation — expressing anger to the other person 对抗：向对方表达愤怒
⚑ Mixed functions; interactive effects with other coping types 功能混合，与其他应对类型有交互效应
Meaning-Focused Coping 意义聚焦应对
Meaning
  • Pearlin & Schooler (1978); Folkman (2008)
  • Drawing on beliefs (religious, spiritual, justice) to motivate coping 借助信念（宗教、精神、公正）驱动应对
  • Drawing on values and existential goals (purpose in life) 借助价值观和存在性目标（人生目的）
  • Helps sustain coping and well-being in difficult times 帮助在困难时期维持应对能力和幸福感
A Closer Look: Positive Reappraisal 正向重评详解
  • A highly adaptive emotion-focused strategy 高度适应性的情绪聚焦策略
  • Involves deriving personally relevant positive meaning from an experience despite its negative qualities 从消极体验中找到个人意义
  • Much more than "looking on the bright side" 不仅仅是"往好处想"
  • Nowlan et al. (2016): PR associated with higher positive emotion, lower anxiety and depression in older adults 正向重评与更多积极情绪、更少焦虑抑郁相关
✓ PR intervention programs show success in reducing anxiety (Ockhuijsen et al., 2014) 干预项目在减少焦虑方面有效
A Closer Look: Disclosure 情绪披露详解
  • An adaptive strategy: describing feelings about a stressful/traumatic experience 适应性策略：描述对压力/创伤体验的感受
  • An important aspect of emotion regulation 情绪调节的重要方面
  • Can be verbal or written 可以是口头或书面
  • Cepeda et al. (2008): cancer patients who wrote with more emotional disclosure had less pain and higher well-being 癌症患者中情感披露更多者疼痛更少、幸福感更高
  • Benefits also found in PTSD (Sloan et al., 2015) and eating disorders (Weber et al., 2006) 在PTSD和饮食障碍中也有获益
Ideally engaged with intent to resolve or work through things 理想情况下应以解决问题为目的
Approach vs. Avoidance — Benefits & Costs 趋近 vs. 回避 优缺点对比
StrategyPossible Benefits 可能的益处Possible Costs 可能的代价
Approach 趋近 Appropriate action; release of emotions; assimilation of trauma; stress reduction 适当行动；情绪释放；整合创伤；减少压力 Increased distress; interference with action; emotional numbness 增加痛苦；干扰行动；情绪麻木
Avoidance 回避 Short-term stress reduction; allows for dosing; increased hope and courage 短期减压；允许分剂量处理；增加希望和勇气 Increased distress long-term; non-productive worry and rumination 长期增加痛苦；无效担忧与反刍
C Resilience & Helpful Traits 韧性与有益人格特质
Definition 定义

Psychological Resilience: Positive adaptation or successful coping after a stressful or adverse situation (Hopf, 2010). 心理韧性：在压力或逆境后的积极适应或成功应对。即从压力/逆境中恢复而不留下持久影响。

Three Pillars of Resilience (Major et al., 1998) 韧性三要素
  • Self-esteem — positive regard for oneself 自尊
  • Personal control — belief in one's ability to influence outcomes 个人控制感
  • Optimism — expectation that good things will happen 乐观主义
✓ Similar to hardiness: control + commitment + challenge 类似于坚韧性：控制+承诺+挑战
Health Outcomes of Resilience in Old Age (MacLeod et al., 2016) 老年韧性的健康结局
  • Improved quality of life 生活质量提升
  • Independence in ADL (activities of daily living) 日常生活独立性
  • Faster cardiovascular recovery 心血管恢复更快
  • Increased longevity & lower mortality risk 寿命增加，死亡风险降低
  • Better mental health; greater happiness & well-being 心理健康更好；幸福感更高
  • Lower rates of depression; higher life satisfaction 抑郁率更低；生活满意度更高
  • Successful aging despite adversities 尽管逆境仍能成功老龄化
Other Helpful Traits 其他有益特质
  • Sense of coherence — seeing life as manageable and meaningful 连贯感：认为生活是可管理且有意义的
  • Optimism — expectation that good things will happen 乐观
  • Low neuroticism / high emotional stability 低神经质/高情绪稳定性
  • High extraversion, agreeableness, conscientiousness 高外向性、宜人性、尽责性
  • High openness to experience 高开放性
  • Type B personality — low competitiveness, low hostility B型人格：低竞争性，低敌意
These traits predict more successful coping across situations 这些特质在各类情境中都预测更成功的应对
Successful Coping — How to Measure 如何衡量成功应对
  • Best indicator = THE OUTCOME 最佳指标 = 结果
  • Was the goal accomplished? 目标是否达成？
  • Was the problem solved? 问题是否解决？
  • Was there an impact on mood or health? 对情绪或健康是否有影响？
  • Was there an impact on the relationship? 对关系是否有影响？
  • Research method: daily process / daily diary studies 研究方法：每日过程/日记研究
D Social Support & Networks 社会支持与社交网络
Key Distinction 关键区别

Social Support = functional content and quality of social relationships. 社会支持 = 社会关系的功能内容与质量

Social Networks = number of social relationships; degree of social integration. 社交网络 = 社会关系的数量；社会整合程度

Most research: quality matters more to health, but quantity DOES still matter (Ozbay et al., 2007). 质量对健康更重要，但数量也有影响

Types of Social Support 社会支持的类型
  • Emotional/Esteem — feeling loved, cared for, valued, respected; empathy 情感/尊重支持：感到被爱、被重视、被尊重；共情
  • Tangible/Instrumental — receiving material aid or assistance 有形/工具性支持：获得物质援助
  • Informational — getting advice and information 信息支持：获得建议和信息
  • Companionship — availability of others to spend time with 陪伴支持：有人可以共度时光
  • Invisible Support (Bolger et al., 2000) — support given without recipient's awareness; often more effective 隐性支持：提供而接受者未察觉；往往更有效
Maladaptive Forms of Support 不良支持形式
  • Protective Buffering — keeping information from someone to "protect" them; avoiding potential negative interaction 保护性缓冲：为"保护"对方而隐瞒信息
  • Solicitousness — expressing concern; helping without request 过度关心：未被请求而提供帮助
  • Both are maladaptive for both parties (Kool et al., 2009; Zniva et al., 2017) 两者对双方均有不良影响
⚠ Not all support is good — these forms can undermine autonomy and increase distress 并非所有支持都是好的——这些形式会削弱自主性
Stress Buffering Hypothesis 压力缓冲假说
  • Social support buffers the impact of stress on health 社会支持缓冲压力对健康的冲击
  • vs. Direct effects model: support benefits health regardless of stress level vs. 直接效应模型：支持无论压力水平如何都有益健康
  • Gellert et al. (2018): in dementia couples, high social support buffered the negative effect of perceived stress on quality of life 在痴呆症夫妇中，高社会支持缓冲了感知压力对生活质量的负面影响
✓ Tend-and-Befriend (Taylor et al., 2000): people also respond to stress with social behaviours; mediated by oxytocin 趋向-交友反应：人们也会以社会行为应对压力，由催产素介导
Benefits of Providing Support 提供支持的益处
  • Research finds health benefits associated with helping/prosocial behaviour 助人行为与健康获益相关
  • Stress-buffering via increased oxytocin (Poulin & Holman, 2013) 通过催产素增加缓冲压力
  • Reduced hypertension (Sneed & Cohen, 2013) 降低高血压
  • Reduced depression (Creaven et al., 2017) 减少抑郁
Social Networks & Health 社交网络与健康
  • Cohen et al. (1997): people with < 4 types of social roles were 4× more likely to catch a cold than those with 6+ roles 社会角色类型少于4种者感冒可能性是6种以上者的4倍
  • LeRoy et al. (2017): subjective loneliness also worsens cold symptoms 主观孤独感也会加重感冒症状
  • Fowler & Christakis (2008): happiness clusters spread up to 3 degrees of separation 幸福感集群传播至多3度人际关系
Similar contagion effects found for health, wealth, beliefs, weight, tastes 类似的传染效应也存在于健康、财富、信念、体重等方面
Risk of Loneliness & Social Media 孤独风险与社交媒体
  • Holt-Lunstad et al. (2015) meta-analysis: increased mortality risk — loneliness +29%, social isolation +26%, living alone +32% 孤独+29%，社会隔离+26%，独居+32%的早逝风险
  • Social media use associated with: loneliness, frustration, lower well-being, depression, negative body image (multiple studies 2012–2020) 社交媒体使用与孤独、沮丧、较低幸福感、抑郁、消极身体形象相关
  • Technology support can help but is less effective than in-person support (Holtzman et al., 2017) 技术支持有帮助，但不如面对面支持有效
Harvard Study on Adult Development 哈佛成人发展研究 (1938–)

The quality of our relationships has a powerful influence on our health. Tending to your relationships is a form of self-care. — Robert Waldinger, Study Director

人际关系的质量对我们的健康有强大影响。照顾好你的人际关系也是一种自我照料。

E Human-Animal Interaction (HAI) 人与动物互动
Key Source 关键文献

Meta-Analysis by Beetz et al. (2012): review of 69 HAI studies — comprehensive evidence base. Beetz等(2012)对69项HAI研究的元分析

Physiological Benefits 生理益处
  • Reduced blood pressure & improved cardiovascular health 降低血压，改善心血管健康
  • Enhanced immune system functioning 增强免疫系统功能
  • Reduced susceptibility to infection 降低感染易感性
  • Improved pain management 改善疼痛管理
  • Lower cortisol output 降低皮质醇输出
  • Increased survival time after heart attack for dog owners 养狗者心脏病发作后存活时间更长
  • Reduced cortisol in healthcare workers after 5 min with therapy dog 医护人员与治疗犬互动5分钟后皮质醇降低
Psychosocial Benefits 心理社会益处
  • Improved mood 情绪改善
  • Lower anxiety and reduced impact of stress 焦虑减少，压力影响降低
  • Lower fear; reduced aggression 恐惧减少；攻击性降低
  • Increased trustworthiness and trust of others 可信度提高，对他人的信任增加
  • HAI reduces the impact of stress on health — stress buffering HAI降低压力对健康的冲击——压力缓冲
Animals vs. Humans as Support 动物 vs. 人类作为支持
  • Allen et al. (2002): pet owners showed lower stress reaction AND faster recovery vs. non-pet owners who had a friend present 宠物主人比有朋友陪同的非宠物主人压力反应更低且恢复更快
  • In married couples: presence of pet attenuated stress more than presence of spouse (based on 2 stress tasks) 在已婚夫妇中，宠物在场比配偶在场更能减轻压力反应
Tasks used: mental arithmetic + putting hand in ice water 压力任务：心算 + 手放冰水
Mechanisms of HAI HAI的机制
  • Beetz et al. (2012): oxytocin system activation plays a key role 催产素系统激活起关键作用
  • HAI leads to higher output of oxytocin in humans HAI导致人类催产素分泌增加
  • Effects of HAI & oxytocin largely overlap → supports its role HAI与催产素的效果高度重叠→支持其作用
  • Oxytocin released in both humans AND animals in response to touch/petting and mutual gaze (Beetz et al., 2012; Fiset & Plourde, 2015) 抚摸和对视均可在人和动物中释放催产素
Current State of Research 当前研究状况
  • HAI boosts learning & engagement in schools (Gee et al., 2017a) HAI促进学校学习和参与度
  • HAI improves health outcomes in older adults (Gee et al., 2017b) HAI改善老年人健康结局
  • Current research: HAI in veterans with PTSD 当前研究：HAI对PTSD退伍军人的效果
⚠ Limitations: lack of standardized measures; small samples; short-term outcomes; pet involvement not controlled; animals not well described 局限：缺乏标准化测量；小样本；短期结果；宠物参与度未控制
Also: Viewing Aquariums 观看水族箱
  • Lower blood pressure/heart rate from viewing an aquarium (DeSchriver & Riddick, 1990) 观看水族箱可降低血压和心率
  • Suggests even indirect animal interaction has physiological benefits 提示间接动物互动也有生理益处
F Nature, Meditation & Mindfulness 自然、冥想与正念
Physical Health Benefits of Greenspace 绿地的身体健康益处
Nature
  • Meta-analysis: 143 studies (Twohig-Bennett & Jones, 2018)
  • Significant reductions in heart rate, diastolic blood pressure, salivary cortisol 显著降低心率、舒张压、唾液皮质醇
  • Decreased incidence of Type 2 diabetes, all-cause mortality, cardiovascular mortality 降低2型糖尿病、全因死亡率、心血管死亡率
  • UBC Study (Fell, 2010): visual presence of wood lowers SNS activation UBC研究：视觉上有木材存在可降低交感神经系统激活
Mechanisms: Why Does Nature Help? 机制：为什么自然有益？
  • Opportunities for physical activity & exercise 体育活动机会
  • Increased social interaction 社会互动增加
  • Exposure to sunlight & associated benefits 阳光照射及相关益处
  • Mitigation of harmful environmental exposure (noise, pollution) 减少有害环境暴露（噪音、污染）
  • "Old friends" hypothesis — increased exposure to microorganisms important for immune development & inflammatory regulation "老朋友"假说：暴露于微生物有益免疫系统发育
Mental Health Benefits 心理健康益处
  • Lower risk of mental illness (De Vries et al., 2003; Grahn & Stigsdotter, 2003) 降低精神疾病风险
  • Lower rumination & reduced depression (Bratman et al., 2015; Shanahan et al., 2016) 减少反刍，降低抑郁
  • Significant impact on positive affect (happiness); small but significant impact on negative affect (Capaldi et al., 2014; McMahan & Estes, 2015) 对积极情绪影响更大；对消极情绪也有小但显著的影响
  • Bratman et al. (2015): 90-min nature walk → lower rumination & decreased activity in brain regions linked to depression vs. urban walk 90分钟自然漫步→减少反刍，减少抑郁相关脑区活动
Tree Cover & Stress / Forest Bathing 树木覆盖与压力 / 森林浴
  • Jiang et al. (2016): positive linear (dose-response) relationship between urban street tree density and stress recovery 城市街道树木密度与压力恢复呈正线性剂量-反应关系
  • Shinrin-Yoku (森林浴) — traditional Japanese practice of immersing oneself in nature 日本传统森林沐浴实践
  • Hansen et al. (2017) meta-analysis: numerous health benefits of forest bathing 森林浴的多种健康益处
  • Qing (2009): improved immune function (↑ natural killer cell activity) after 3-day shinrin-yoku 3天森林浴后免疫功能改善（自然杀伤细胞活性↑）
Nature deprivation (Louv — "nature-deficit disorder"): urbanized lifestyle decreases nature contact → negative mental health outcomes 自然缺失：城市化生活方式减少自然接触→负面心理健康结局
Meditation 冥想
Mindfulness
  • Broadly refers to practices that induce a calm, peaceful, or relaxed state 广义上指产生平静、和平或放松状态的各种实践
  • Suggested to create a "relaxation response" as alternative to stress response (Benson, 1984) 被认为能产生"放松反应"作为压力反应的替代
  • Alleviates stress, reduces blood pressure, enhances immune function (Barnes et al., 2004; Davidson et al., 2003; Jain et al., 2007) 减轻压力，降低血压，增强免疫功能
Mindfulness 正念
MBT / MBSR
  • Intentionally focused awareness of one's immediate inner and outer experiences 对内外即时体验的有意识关注
  • Moment-by-moment attention to thoughts, emotions, sensations, surroundings 对思想、情绪、感觉、周围环境的逐刻注意
  • Lack of judgment; simple observation 不加评判；简单观察
  • Combined successfully with CBT 与CBT成功结合
Mindfulness Research Evidence 正念研究证据
  • Khoury et al. (2013): MBT meta-analysis, 209 studies, 12,145 participants — moderately effective for depression, anxiety, stress; comparable to CBT MBT元分析：对抑郁、焦虑、压力中等有效；与CBT效果相当
  • Khoury et al. (2015): MBSR meta-analysis, 29 studies, 2,668 healthy individuals — moderately effective for stress, depression, anxiety, distress; improves quality of life MBSR元分析：对健康个体的压力、抑郁、焦虑中等有效；改善生活质量
✓ How to practice: set aside time to observe the moment; body scan; focus on breathing; observe thoughts without judgment 如何练习：留出时间观察当下；身体扫描；专注呼吸；不加评判地观察思想
L-3 Health Tips (From the Lecture) 本讲健康建议

① Accept the things you cannot change. 接受无法改变的事情

② Change the things you can. 改变可以改变的事情

③ Put your relationships to good use. 善用你的人际关系

④ Talk to animals, hug trees, live near water — and meditate. 与动物交流，拥抱树木，住近水边——冥想
